Privacy Policy for Focus Orbit

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Published by: Raizel Tech India ("we", "us")

Contact: raizeltechindia@gmail.com

Focus Orbit is a number-tapping game for Android. This policy explains what

information the game uses and who receives it. We wrote it to be short and

specific to what the app actually does.

1. What stays on your phone

Focus Orbit has no account, no sign-up and no login. Your game progress (levels

cleared, stars, scores, Focus Rating, daily-challenge history and streak) is

saved only on your device. We do not receive it and cannot see it. If you

uninstall the game or clear its storage in Android settings, this data is deleted.

2. Advertising

Focus Orbit is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show ads, measure

them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects and sends

to Google:

  • your IP address (which can indicate your approximate location)
  • app interactions such as app launches, taps and ad views
  • diagnostic information such as app performance and crash data
  • device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID where it is available

This data is sent to Google over an encrypted connection. We do not receive it as

personal information and we do not sell it. How Google uses it is described in

Google's own policies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and

https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions where the

law requires it, the game shows Google's consent form when you first open it.

You can change your choice later from the "Privacy choices" button on the home

screen when it is shown.

  • On Android you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation

in Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by phone).

  • Some ads are optional rewarded ads: you choose to watch one to get a bonus,

for example to continue a round. You can always decline.

3. Children

Focus Orbit is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly

collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used the

game in a way that concerns you, contact us at the address above.

4. What we do not do

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone

or precise location. We do not use the game to collect health information, and

the game makes no health claims.

5. Changes

If we add features that change how data is used, for example online

leaderboards or sign-in, we will update this policy and the in-app information

before those features are released. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
